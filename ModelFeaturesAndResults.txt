Going with cats theres about 3~4 types of trades,
1. bots trading at margins (3,4,5,6) (99%+)
2. Noise (-1) which comprises over 50% of users characterized by high average price (~60%) probably to keep it safe though high std, very chaotic, higher-ish quartiles of nMarkets
3. Low price trades (1)
4. Lowish max spend (2) Second most populous group with ~50% winrate
5. oddballs? (0) lower mean price than 2 but higher mean win_rate
